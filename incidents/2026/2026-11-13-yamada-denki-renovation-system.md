The potentially affected fields include names, addresses, telephone numbers, renovation histories and estimates. The 3.5 million figure counts potentially affected records. The notice excludes My Number identifiers, credit card data and purchases unrelated to renovation, including consumer electronics purchases in stores and online. The initial access method and any data extraction remain under investigation.
