Giftee uses Prismatix to manage consumer member data and authenticate sign-ins. The attack path, password storage format and affected member count remain undisclosed. The November 13 update identifies email addresses and password information as possibly exposed. Giftee says it is working with Prismatix on investigation and recovery and will publish the reopening date once it verifies safety.
