LALIGA's November 23 notice confirms theft of registered fans' personal data from a league-managed platform also used by some clubs. The reported intrusion ran from November 14 to 16. LALIGA says it stopped extraction and blocked the attacker on November 16, with no effect on its other systems or the competition. These are the league's containment and scope statements; the number of affected people, complete field inventory and technical entry mechanism remain undisclosed.

The club notices describe identification and contact data. CD Eldense says its information at the time excludes passwords and banking/payment data. Leganés says it is not aware of publication or fraudulent use of the stolen data at its notice date. Those bounded assessments do not reverse the confirmed extraction or establish that every fan account is unaffected. The clubs largely relay the same LALIGA notification; these pages do not constitute independent technical investigations of the intrusion.

LALIGA reports notifying affected clubs and Spain's AEPD and filing a complaint with the National Police's cybercrime unit. Scope analysis and increased monitoring continue. No CVE, software patch, attacker identity, ransom amount or completed compensation is established by these notices. L4 reflects confirmed intrusion and data extraction across a shared fan-service platform, not a fabricated victim count or an assertion that each club's own infrastructure was breached.

The practical risk identified by the clubs is impersonation through email, text messages or calls. Follow the official contact routes for the club and service you actually used. A containment announcement does not erase copied data or authenticate later messages claiming to offer remediation.

CD Tenerife’s official notice confirms that its fans are among those affected. It describes the identification/contact-data scope as preliminary and subject to revision, says it is notifying affected fans individually by email, and reports that the club has reviewed its own accesses and controls. The club and LALIGA notified AEPD. Normal service operation does not establish that copied personal data has been recovered or deleted. Tenerife does not supply a victim count or confirm banking/password theft; Eldense’s narrower exclusions must not be generalized to every club.
