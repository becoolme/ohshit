Queens Public Library confirmed that ransomware disrupted the network serving all its locations. Branches stayed open, although some programs could be affected. The first confirmation covered the website, mobile app, on-site computers, digital media services, self-service checkout and return machines, and public Wi-Fi. The later update restored machines and Wi-Fi at open locations; it did not announce full recovery.

The library has not publicly identified the entry point, ransomware group, a recovery deadline or the scope of any patron-data access. L3 reflects the disruption to a public library network across all locations. The outage does not establish theft of patron records or a compromise of a particular vendor.

QPL published its [initial confirmation](https://x.com/QPLNYC/status/2118128693914874122) and [restoration update](https://x.com/QPLNYC/status/2118874433838305413) on its official social account. The cited public copies preserve both threads.
