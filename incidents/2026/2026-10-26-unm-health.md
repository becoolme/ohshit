UNM says the incident forced ambulance diversion and canceled outpatient care. That clinical disruption warrants L4. Byod claims a much broader intrusion and data theft; UNM instead says core systems were not penetrated. Neither a patient-data total nor ransomware attribution is confirmed.

On October 28, UNM confirmed classes and research had resumed, with clinical operations fully functional, and said it made no concessions to the group behind the threat. Its warning about follow-up scams does not confirm the attackers' data-theft claims.
