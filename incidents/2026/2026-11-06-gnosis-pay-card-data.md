The disclosure concerns customer information in the legacy consumer-card system. Gnosis Pay says its separate enterprise white-label card platform and user funds were unaffected. The public notice does not establish the exact fields, a victim total, confirmed fraudulent spending, stolen seed phrases or a wallet-draining attack. Ask about your own records; a customer’s public screenshot does not define every customer’s exposure.

The company says it notified every affected customer, replaced credentials and addressed the vulnerability; it is also reviewing the incident and arranging specialist misuse monitoring. These are vendor statements, not an independent audit or proof that copied data was recovered. L4 reflects confirmed customer-data exposure in a payment platform, with scale still unknown. This November event is separate from the June 2026 Zodiac-module funds exploit; do not import that event’s loss or wallet totals.
