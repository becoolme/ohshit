LifeCard initially disclosed possible customer-data exposure and a partial service suspension on October 31. Its November 2 follow-up confirms that data was stolen at its V-Preca contractor, **GMO Payment Gateway**, and lists **69 affected accounts**, not a count of unique people:

- **63 accounts:** names, email addresses, telephone numbers and partial addresses.
- **3 accounts:** V-Preca numbers, V-Preca security codes, expiry dates and names.
- **3 accounts:** partial V-Preca numbers.

LifeCard says the three accounts with full card credentials were closed on October 31 and that it had found no misuse of those three accounts or secondary harm from this incident. It will notify affected customers individually. The theft of information is now confirmed; theft of balances is not established.

The investigation follow-up still describes a partial suspension. A separate November 2 notice confirms that the suspended service **has resumed** after LifeCard completed the checks and measures it considered necessary; the maintenance FAQ now says there is no maintenance. The notices give no restoration time or technical root cause. Restoration does not undo the data exposure or establish that the investigation has concluded.

The original notice also promised accommodations for postpaid-charge repayments, with details to follow. Verify any account-specific repayment or recovery instructions through official support. Broader LifeCard or payment-network compromise is not established.
