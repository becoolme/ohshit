L4 reflects confirmed personal-data exposure at a telecom provider, with scale unknown. M1’s public release corroborates the fields reported from customer notices. It identifies October 30 as the detection date and says services and operations continued throughout. Its assessment excludes payment-card data, financial or banking information and account access. M1 plans to notify affected customers by email or letter and says it is working with authorities and outside specialists. No CVE, software patch, compensation scheme or blanket password-reset order is established. This is separate from the Doctor Anywhere disclosure on the same day.
