Dexcom says the attacker was able to download certain files after gaining access to employee accounts. It revoked the compromised accounts’ access and reports no evidence of ongoing unauthorized activity or misuse. The notices give no total affected count. They describe exposure associated with corporate accounts and do not establish compromise of glucose monitors or their software.

The identity-data notice lists possible exposure of names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth and government identification information. The health-data notice lists names, addresses and potentially pharmacy, provider, birth date, prescription or diagnosis information, including whether someone uses Dexcom. That letter excludes Social Security numbers and financial information for its recipients and lists (844) 971-8641 for questions.
