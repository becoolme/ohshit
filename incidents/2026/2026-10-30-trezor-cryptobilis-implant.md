The customer reports an added board between the main board and screen, using cellular connectivity to transmit displayed backups. Trezor has reviewed photos and interviewed the buyer; the device is still awaiting examination. The precaution covers all CryptoBilis-supplied models, rather than all Trezor wallets. L3 reflects a mainstream wallet warning; neither theft nor the reported mechanism is confirmed. No attacker identity is established.
