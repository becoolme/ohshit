MPOWERHealth says the unauthorized party reached a limited part of its internal network. The company does not disclose the entry method or a victim total. Potentially affected fields differ by person and include names, birth dates, Social Security numbers, driver’s license or government identification numbers, financial account information, and medical or insurance data. The company reports no evidence of medical-information misuse.
