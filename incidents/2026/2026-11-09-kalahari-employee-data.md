Kalahari says the October 9 network intrusion led to an October 16 determination that personal information may have been affected. The sample addressed to employees lists a date of birth among the possible fields. The Texas public row lists names, addresses and Social Security numbers. The state figure covers Texans. Kalahari did not describe the access path or say whether data was copied, and the notice reports no evidence of specific misuse at the time it was written.
