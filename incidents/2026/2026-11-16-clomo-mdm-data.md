i Cubed Systems detected suspicious access on November 2 in systems that operate services including CLOMO MDM, its enterprise mobile device management service. The company later confirmed that some information customers had registered in CLOMO MDM leaked. It is investigating the cause and scope and has withheld the entry method, data fields and number of affected customers. As of its disclosure, the vendor had seen no confirmed misuse or impact on its other products or services.

The vendor says affected organizations get details and instructions at their registered system administrator address. It blocked the access route it identified and kept the service running under increased monitoring.
