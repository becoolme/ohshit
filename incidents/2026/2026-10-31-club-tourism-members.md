Potential fields include names, nationality, gender, birth date/age, addresses, phones, email, membership IDs and emergency contacts. The operator excludes passwords, passports, cards and bank-account numbers. Millions of potentially exposed records justify L4; this is not a confirmed count of unique victims.

The company reports isolation, blocking and corrective work, with investigation continuing. No specific secondary harm is confirmed.
