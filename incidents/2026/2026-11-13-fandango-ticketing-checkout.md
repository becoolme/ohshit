Fandango says it investigated with outside cybersecurity specialists, resolved the checkout issue and enhanced its protections. It has disclosed the transaction window and the data that may have been accessible. The company has given no technical cause, evidence of a checkout skimmer or overall affected count. Fandango says it was unaware of fraudulent use resulting from this incident when it issued the notice.

The sample notice excludes names, government identifiers, Social Security numbers and other account information. This is rated L3 because it involves a widely used ticketing checkout and potential exposure of the full card details from some transactions.
