The confirmed access period is July 30–November 12, not proof of uninterrupted daily access. The agency is working with police and external specialists and has reported to Japan’s Personal Information Protection Commission. It reports no identified secondary personal-data harm so far. File counts, data categories, affected people, entry mechanism and restoration date are not established in the notice. Do not merge this case with September’s GSS incident or infer that all GSS systems were compromised.
