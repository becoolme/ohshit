Infcurion’s first notice confirms an actual intrusion and external disclosure of customer personal information in Wallet Station. It does not identify a vulnerability, entry path, exposed data fields, affected-person total or financial loss. Its statement that no resulting misuse has been observed does not mean that no data was stolen.

The company says suspicious communications have been blocked, affected functions stopped and service temporarily suspended for affected providers. It continues monitoring providers it considers unaffected. Do not expand this into a shutdown of every provider or a confirmed recovery. Investigation, root-cause analysis and prevention work continue; restart depends on security checks and consultation with providers.

This payment-platform incident warrants an operational warning, but the initial notice does not establish a mass victim count. It is distinct from [Infcurion’s downstream Notion AI exposure through Fireworks](/incidents/fireworks-cloud-customer-data/); that earlier notice’s exclusion of Infcurion-operated services does not cover this new intrusion. Nothing in these notices establishes that the incidents share an attacker or an entry path.
