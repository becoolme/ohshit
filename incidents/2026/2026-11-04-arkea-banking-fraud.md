Crédit Mutuel Arkéa’s November 4 company release confirms a cyberattack that enabled fraudulent transactions. It says it contacted competent authorities and supervisors, mobilized its teams and temporarily suspended some transactions. It calls the incident contained/closed and reports progressive operational resumption during the morning after checks and stronger safeguards; its teams continue evaluating consequences and strengthening security. These are the bank’s statements, not an independent forensic conclusion or proof that every transfer has completed.

The bank says customer holdings across its federations and subsidiaries are unaffected and that some transfers were delayed. It has not disclosed an affected-customer count, detailed attack window, entry point, compromised software version, data-exposure scope or quantified loss. It says the amount is still being assessed and does not threaten the group’s financial strength. The release does not identify Fortuneo as the intrusion point or confirm ownership of wallets cited in earlier crypto reports. Transfers on a blockchain cannot establish this bank’s loss, the source of funds or the attack mechanism; no crypto-flow estimate is adopted here as a bank-confirmed amount.

L4 reflects the bank’s confirmation of real cyber-enabled financial fraud and transaction interruption. Customers have no published software patch to install. Follow the bank’s own service and adviser instructions, continue watching for phishing, and distinguish progressive recovery from completed investigation or independent verification of all balances.
