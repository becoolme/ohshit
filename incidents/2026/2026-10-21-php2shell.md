Two bugs in PHP's own engine, not in some framework or plugin. **Nearly every PHP release back to 5.4 is
affected** by at least one of them. Apache `mod_php` is the highest-risk setup, but PHP-FPM, `php-cgi`,
FrankenPHP and LiteSpeed are all in scope.

The disclosure was planned for October 22. On the evening of October 20 a proof-of-concept video from
Calif, one of the reporting teams, went public by accident, and PHP pushed its releases out on October 21
at 14:00 UTC. So for once, defenders and attackers started the race at the same time.

**Is it exploited?** It depends on whom you ask. Patchstack's partners and watchTowr saw real attack
attempts within hours. Cloudflare saw early attempts too. Sophos had no reports of exploitation, and
nobody had confirmed a successful break-in at the time of writing. We grade by the worst credible case:
public PoC + default configuration + basically the whole web = **L5**.

### Common misreading

Turning off `session.upload_progress` is **not** a fix. It only cuts the confirmed path to
CVE-2026-109619. The out-of-memory bug, CVE-2026-109618, does not need it.
