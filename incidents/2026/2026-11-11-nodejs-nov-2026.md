This is a separate batch from the [October releases](/incidents/nodejs-oct-2026/). The bulletin lists seven high, six medium and three low severity Node.js flaws. The existing October entry tracks the previously disclosed wildcard permission flaw. The November bulletin also lists llhttp, undici and sqlite updates on specified lines. CVE-2026-123864 requires a Node.js HTTPS handler that flushes response headers before the request body ends and later ends the response with a non-empty chunk. A route that reflects or stores request bodies can expose the resulting memory. Cleartext node:http is outside this path. Other bugs affect HTTP/2 over TLS or cleartext, TLS handshakes, mTLS policy or local feature use. Use the bulletin’s per-CVE applicability for prioritization and patch all supported lines.
