Snow Peak attributes the first three cases to vulnerabilities in the respective services. Its November 13 figures are separate scopes.

- Resale buyback: 24,948 customers in scope. Possible fields include identity and contact details, membership and customer identifiers, identity verification status, orders and transfer account details. Bank PINs, banking logins, passwords and card numbers are excluded.
- Events: 5,923 people’s information may have been exposed, including applicant and companion details, contact data and booking details. Card or password exposure has not been confirmed.
- Community app: 21,553 people in scope. Possible fields are membership number, display name, biography and cover image. Contact details, passwords and card data are excluded.
- Store and membership app: up to 445 accounts with unauthorized logins. Snow Peak suspects externally obtained credentials. Profile data, delivery details, purchase history, points, card type and the first six and last two digits could have been viewed. Full card numbers are excluded and the company reports no password leak from its systems in this case.
