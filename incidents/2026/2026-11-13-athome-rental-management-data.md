At Home says the records may include names, birth dates, phone numbers, addresses and contract data such as building names, unit numbers and contract status. The company has not confirmed any bank-account data leak, and the system does not hold credit card data. At disclosure it had not confirmed publication, misuse or impact on its other services.

The company says it blocked access from the source, is reviewing logs and is working to notify affected parties individually. The entry path and the amount actually taken remain under investigation.
