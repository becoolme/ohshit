The spokesperson says no evidence of a breach affecting Commission staff as a whole has been found so far. This does not rule out a narrower exposure. Many elements are contained, but the Commission declines to declare the campaign over. No named AI model, attribution, ransom demand or worldwide victim total is established. L3 reflects confirmed disruption and an ongoing institutional response, without a proven mass data leak.
