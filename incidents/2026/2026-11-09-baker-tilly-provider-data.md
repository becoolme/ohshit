The company’s letter confirms an outside party obtained data from the provider’s platform. It does not name the provider or identify how access began. The [California register](https://oag.ca.gov/ecrime/databreach/reports/sb24-632686) gives September 2 as a breach date. Baker Tilly says it learned of the incident September 6 and identified whose information was affected on October 28. The Texas public row lists names, addresses, Social Security numbers and an unspecified other category. The public sample uses placeholders for individual fields. The state counts above apply only to their respective jurisdictions. The reviewed public records do not establish a verified overall count. Baker Tilly says it notified law enforcement and had no known related identity theft or fraud when it issued the notice.
