EGL Holdings says its technology vendor reported on November 13 that an unauthorized person had viewed some EGL customer data. The vendor has not been named. The company said on November 16 that it had not received the final investigation report and had not established the number of affected customers.

EGL lists names, phone numbers, email addresses, identity card numbers, travel document numbers, birth dates and some itinerary details as potentially involved. EGL says credit card data was outside the reported scope and it had seen no evidence of misuse. The company strengthened security controls and monitoring, further masked customer data and said affected systems had returned to normal operation. It plans to notify affected customers.
